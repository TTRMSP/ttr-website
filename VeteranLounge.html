Plan a Flagship Build

Pricing structure: Cost of Goods + 15% covers logistics, travel, shipping, deployment, setup, installation, and configuration.

Optional Add-Ons

  • Private telehealth booth
  • Additional PC stations
  • Additional consoles / displays
  • Theater or projector system
  • Surround audio
  • Streaming equipment
  • Managed IT support
  • Security cameras / access control

Internet Requirements

Tier 1 250 Mbps up/down min.
Tier 2 500 Mbps up/down min.
Tier 3 1 Gbps up/down min.

Wired connections are recommended for all stations.

Partnership Requirement

Branded Recognition

Participating Posts allow placement of a branded banner or marketing display featuring Always Give Back Charity, Total Tech Resources MSP, and the participating Post number/location. Both parties sign a media use release before the project begins.

Important Notes

  • Required permits must be obtained before project start.
  • Additional services, upgrades, and system management are quoted separately.
  • All build-outs require standard 110-volt power outlets; dedicated circuits preferred where possible.
  • Room size, electrical capacity, internet access, shipping & availability may affect final pricing.

Sponsorship Friendly & Custom Built

Each build-out can support local sponsorship opportunities — giving businesses, donors, veteran supporters, and community partners a clear way to help fund the project while receiving approved recognition through signage, media coverage, and project documentation. Every deployment is tailored to your Post's space, budget, electrical capacity, internet, veteran participation, and long-term program goals. Deployment is scheduled around travel, equipment sourcing, and the build calendar — early planning is recommended.
