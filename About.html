Who We Are

An outsourced IT department that learns your business, not just your devices.

TTR was built around a simple idea, businesses should not have to choose between deep technical experience and personal service. We work as an extension of your company, supporting the people who use the technology while helping leadership manage the bigger picture.

Founder led, experience driven.

Total Tech Resources is led by a technology professional with more than 30 years of hands-on experience across support, hardware, infrastructure and business technology. That experience shapes how TTR approaches problems today, look at the entire environment, find the real cause, explain the options clearly and build a solution around the business.

We are not trying to be another faceless help desk. The goal is to become a team your employees recognize, your leadership can reach and your business can rely on as technology becomes more important to everything you do.

Your business should never have to wonder who owns the technology problem. With TTR, there is one team accountable for helping move it forward.

What TTR is built to be

Not a collection of disconnected services, one technology relationship that brings everyday support and long-term planning together.

  • A clear place for employees to get help
  • A technology partner leadership can talk to directly
  • One team coordinating systems, vendors and infrastructure
  • Support that understands how your business actually works
  • Planning that looks beyond the next ticket
Why TTR Is Different

You are not hiring a ticket queue. You are gaining an IT team.

The value of managed IT is not simply having someone answer when something breaks. It is having a team that understands the environment well enough to prevent problems, coordinate the moving pieces and help the business make better technology decisions.

Personal Accountability

Your business is not just an account number. TTR is built around relationships, familiarity and clear ownership of the technology conversation.

The Whole Environment

Users, devices, Microsoft 365, networks, vendors, backups and infrastructure are connected. We manage them with that bigger picture in mind.

Clear Communication

Leadership should understand what matters, why it matters and what comes next without needing to become technology experts themselves.

Built For What Comes Next

We help plan hardware, infrastructure, vendors and priorities so growth does not turn every technology decision into an emergency.
