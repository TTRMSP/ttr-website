Built for veterans

TTR builds the custom computers provided to veterans through Always Give Back. Community donations help make those machines available at no cost to recipients.

More ways to connect

A computer can help a veteran access education, employment, telehealth, creative work and gaming communities. The mission reaches beyond hardware to human connection.

Help the next build happen

Donations support the parts, supplies, shipping and setup behind veteran builds. Visit Always Give Back to learn about donating, volunteering or partnering with the charity.

Learn more about the mission at alwaysgiveback.org. Donations are handled through Always Give Back’s Givebutter campaign.
